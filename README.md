## 🔎 What Is Circuit Companion 2026?

Circuit Companion 2026 is a **desktop reference environment** for people who repair, reverse-map, or simply study consumer electronics at the component level. It does not pretend to be magic. It does not promise to "fix everything." Instead, it does something more honest and more useful: it organizes the enormous, messy universe of hardware documentation into something a human brain can actually navigate at 2 a.m. with a soldering iron warming up beside them.

The project bundles three fundamental ideas into a single workspace:

1. **A schematic-aware viewer** — pan, zoom, annotate, and cross-reference board diagrams without fighting your file explorer.
2. **A curated hardware data index** — component identifiers, board revisions, and common failure signatures gathered from community submissions and public service documentation.
3. **A diagnostic companion mode** — guided reasoning prompts that help you move from "it doesn't turn on" to "ah, it's the rail regulator" without skipping steps.

It is important to be crystal clear about what this is and what it is not. Circuit Companion is a **reference and reasoning aid**. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any commercial repair toolkit brand. It is an original, independent initiative built in the spirit of open knowledge exchange.

The 2026 edition represents a substantial rewrite of the underlying indexing engine, a fresh visual identity, and a genuine leap in how quickly large schematic files open on modest hardware.

## 🎭 The Metaphor Behind the Madness

Imagine a library where every book is a circuit board, every page is a net, and every footnote is a capacitor with opinions. That library exists — it's just never been catalogued properly. Most technicians build that catalogue in their heads over a decade. Circuit Companion 2026 tries to build it in software, so newcomers don't have to spend their first five years lost in the stacks.

We think of the app less as a "tool" and more as a **nautical chart for a sea of copper**. Charts don't sail the ship. They don't catch the fish. But try crossing the ocean without one and see how that goes.

## ✨ Feature Highlights

- **🗺️ Schematic Navigator** — Open large, multi-layer diagram files with smooth panning, layer toggling, and annotation pins that persist between sessions.
- **🔗 Net Cross-Referencing** — Click a net name and see every place it appears across the loaded schematic set.
- **🧮 Component Lookup Assistance** — Type a marking code and receive candidate identifications ranked by context, package, and frequency of community reports.
- **📚 Reference Library Indexing** — A local, searchable catalogue of board revisions, connector pinouts, and service notes you supply or import.
- **🧠 Guided Diagnostic Flows** — Step-by-step reasoning templates for common symptom families: no power, no display, random shutdown, charging faults, and audio path failures.
- **🖊️ Annotation Layer** — Leave notes for your future self, your colleague, or the poor soul who inherits this repair ticket next month.
- **📤 Export & Share** — Produce clean annotated snapshots for a work log, a client report, or a forum post.
- **🌗 Light & Dark Themes** — Because schematics at 3 a.m. deserve mercy.
- **⚡ Snappy Load Times** — Indexed file loading means you spend more time diagnosing and less time waiting.
- **🧭 Session Memory** — Reopen the app and land exactly where you left off, down to the zoom level.

## 🖥️ Responsive UI — Designed for Every Screen

Not everyone repairs on a 34-inch ultrawide. Some of us are hunched over a 13-inch laptop on a kitchen table. Some of us have a secondary monitor entirely devoted to a PDF that refuses to scroll smoothly. Circuit Companion 2026 treats **layout fluidity as a first-class feature**, not an afterthought.

- Adaptive panel docking that collapses gracefully on narrow displays.
- Touch-friendly hit targets for technicians using pen input or tablets running desktop Windows.
- Scalable iconography that stays legible from 100% to 250% display scaling.
- Keyboard-first navigation for power users who never want to touch the mouse mid-diagnosis.
- A distraction-free "focus mode" that dims everything except the schematic canvas.

The goal is simple: **the interface should disappear when you're deep in a trace**, and reappear instantly when you need it.

## 🌍 Multilingual Support

## 🗂️ Reference Library & Schematic Index

The heart of Circuit Companion is its **index**. A file system is a pile of documents; an index is a map of a pile. The 2026 index engine supports:

- Full-text search across titles, descriptions, board names, and user tags.
- Faceted filtering by device family, board revision, connector type, and symptom category.
- Duplicate detection, so your library doesn't slowly fill with seventeen copies of the same diagram.
- Import from folders, archives, and structured manifests you author yourself.
- Custom tag vocabularies for teams that want a shared language.
- Index health reports that flag unreadable files, orphaned references, and stale entries.

Everything is stored **locally by default**. The library is yours. It lives on your machine, under your control, and it works with or without a network connection.

## 🔍 Diagnostic Workflow Assistance

This is where Circuit Companion stops being a filing cabinet and starts being a colleague.

Diagnostic flows are **structured reasoning templates**, not automated conclusions. Each flow walks you through a sequence of observations and measurements, asking questions like:

- "With the device unplugged, what resistance do you measure across the primary rail?"
- "Does the symptom persist when the display assembly is disconnected?"
- "Which rails come up first, and in what order?"

Based on your answers, the flow narrows the candidate space and suggests the next most informative measurement. It never claims certainty. It simply reduces the size of the haystack. Experienced technicians often move faster than the flows — that's fine. The flows exist for the moments when you've been staring at the same board for two hours and need fresh eyes in software form.

## ⚙️ Performance & Footprint

Circuit Companion is engineered around a principle we call **"the technician's patience budget."** Every second spent waiting for software is a second not spent looking at a board. So the team obsesses over:

- **Cold start under a few seconds** on a mid-range laptop.
- **Incremental indexing** so adding one file doesn't rebuild the whole library.
- **Memory-mapped schematic loading** to keep large diagrams responsive.
- **Lazy rendering** of off-screen regions, so panning never stutters.
- **Background maintenance** tasks that pause the instant you interact with the app.

The installer itself is deliberately compact. No bundled runtimes you don't need, no telemetry daemons, no mysterious background services. What you see in the task manager is what you get.

## 🔐 Security & Privacy Posture

A reference tool should be a vault, not a funnel. Circuit Companion 2026 makes the following commitments:

- **No telemetry by default.** Nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly export it.
- **Offline-first architecture.** The entire application is functional without an internet connection.
- **Local-only storage** for libraries, annotations, and preferences.
- **No third-party analytics SDKs.** None. Zero.
- **Signed releases** with published checksums so you can verify what you're running.
- **Responsible disclosure policy** — security reports are acknowledged within 72 hours.

If a future feature ever requires network access, it will be opt-in, clearly labelled, and independently revocable.

## 💬 Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Circuit Companion a replacement for my existing repair suite?**
No. It's a companion. It complements whatever you already use. Many technicians run it side-by-side with their primary tools.

**Does it work without an internet connection?**
Yes, entirely. The core experience is offline-first. Network features are optional add-ons.

**Can I import my own documents?**
Absolutely. The library is designed to accept your existing collection. You are not locked into any format we control.

**Is there a mobile version?**
Not natively. The desktop app scales down to small laptop screens, but a dedicated mobile build is not on the near-term roadmap.

**How do I report a bug?**
Open an issue in this repository with your OS build, a description of the symptom, and steps to reproduce. Screenshots and log excerpts help enormously.

**Is my data shared with anyone?**
No. Nothing leaves your machine unless you export it yourself. There is no hidden sync.

**Do you accept translations?**
Yes, enthusiastically. Localisation contributions are among the most valuable things a community can offer a project like this.

**What if I find inaccurate reference data?**
Tell us. Every correction makes the index more trustworthy for the next person. Accuracy is a collective responsibility.
